What inkora is about

We're a local Melbourne business dedicated to helping people bring their ideas to life. Whether it's a feature wall at home, a custom mural for a café, or a large logo in an office reception, we love turning empty spaces into something meaningful, memorable, and uniquely yours

From residential projects to commercial branding, we work closely with every client to deliver custom wall and floor printing solutions that combine creativity, quality, and attention to detail. No project is too small when it comes to making a big impact.
